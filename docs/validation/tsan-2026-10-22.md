# ThreadSanitizer validation and performance observations

## Source scope

The final PR is based on upstream `main` at `29a865648a585d710fd4a526e21b104691481406`, preserving its IO and JET changes. Its 57 task paths combine clean C13 with the final LP prefilter (E), excluding unrelated concurrent owner work. Performance measurements below use frozen source snapshots; they do not benchmark the exact rebased PR or every configuration.

## Changes

- Synchronize shared queue, packed gain-cache, label and weight accesses; read compact-map keys and values from one coherent observation.
- Preserve concurrent Growt synchronization and restrict ordinary map reads to immutable phases after writers join. Distributed foreign weights retain stable indices and fresh atomic payload reads.
- Reuse immutable adjacency counts for eligible unit-edge degree queries while retaining weighted atomic fallback and fresh connectivity reads.
- Skip weight queries for strictly losing LP gains only in the opted-in clustering/refinement configurations. Equal-gain RNG, fresh potential-winner weights and subsequent move-capacity rechecks remain unchanged.

Regression coverage includes concurrent data structures, packed widths, cache lifecycle, MPI phases, and LP selection/RNG behavior.

## Correctness validation

Final PR full-suite TSAN result: **506/506 passed** (455 non-MPI entries and 51 MPI entries), with zero TSAN reports or fatals and no suppressions. The macOS Apple Clang/MPICH build enabled distributed and experimental features, 64-bit edge IDs and weights. All 298 actual compiled translation units, including 38 oneTBB units, carried ThreadSanitizer instrumentation. All 719 tracked input bytes and modes remained unchanged throughout the build and suite.

Historical clean C13 passed **444/444** sanitizer entries; a separate owner-inclusive C13 snapshot passed **511/511**, both without TSAN reports or suppressions. These counts precede E and are not substituted for the final PR result. E subsequently passed nine focused MPI entries, three new focused GTests and 12 exact serial label/next-RNG comparisons at one/four ranks. Later owner FM edits are outside those captured validations.

## Performance

Job **84388** completed all **736** owner-inclusive calls within the upper balance limit: 584 full-matrix calls, 80 eco calls and 72 controls. Full-matrix runtime geometric ratios were **1.0171 / 1.0070 / 1.0095 / 1.0186** for default/k16, default/k32768, fast/k16 and fast/k32768. The measured overhead was accepted; equivalence was not established.

Job **84427** separately compared baseline B, C13 C and prefilter E: default, 4 ranks × 8 threads, k16, seed116, three 32-bit-ID graphs, one call per source/graph.

| Graph | B/C/E seconds | B/C/E cuts |
|---|---|---|
| Bump_2911 | 3.34 / 3.37 / 3.49 | 2156484 / 2127221 / 2063899 |
| com-lj.ungraph | 5.58 / 5.96 / 5.76 | 7025447 / 7103748 / 7011553 |
| hollywood-2011 | 6.48 / 6.78 / 5.80 | 26197115 / 25087985 / 24697802 |

All nine calls were upper-valid; lower feasibility was unreported. E/C runtime geometric and summed ratios were **0.949562 / 0.934202**; E/B ratios were **0.988341 / 0.977273**. Bump remains **4.49% slower** than B and com-lj **3.23% slower**. All three E cuts were lower in this sample. Single-call points, changed trajectories and inclusive phase timers establish neither runtime equivalence nor a systematic quality improvement; studies are not pooled.
